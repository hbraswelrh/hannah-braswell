## Hi, I'm Hannah

Hannah is an Associate Product Security Engineer at Red Hat, focusing on proactively securing complex open-source systems. With a B.S. in Computer Engineering from NC State University, she brings a deep background in microarchitecture and embedded systems to her work in the open-source ecosystem.

As an active contributor to several projects and Working Groups within the OpenSSF, she is passionate about pragmatic development and using automation to enhance security workflows. She currently serves as the Community Manager for the OpenSSF Gemara Project and excels at making technical concepts digestible for all audiences.

## Open Source & Community

Hannah is an [OpenSSF Ambassador](https://openssf.org/blog/2026/05/21/introducing-the-first-cohort-of-the-openssf-ambassador-program/), part of the inaugural cohort, passionate about technical evangelism and engaging across open source communities. She believes in developer advocacy and making highly technical concepts available through storytelling and enablement.
